Public packs are listed at **[frostdev.io/kiln/crucible](/kiln/crucible)**. Browsing needs no account.

## Find a pack

Search by name, mod or creator, and narrow the list with the filters: tags, the game build, where it runs (your game, a
dedicated server, a server pack), and how many mods it has. **Sort** puts the newest or the best liked first. The
address keeps your search, so you can share it.

![The Crucible gallery: search, filters and pack cards](/projects/kiln/shots/crucible-web-browse.webp)

Got a code from a friend? Type it into **Got a code?** at the top of any Crucible page.

## A pack's page

Each pack has a page with its cover, creator, summary, download size, game build, the Kiln version it needs and where it
runs, then:

- **Open in Kiln**: the launcher opens the pack's preview ([installing a pack](/kiln/wiki/crucible-install)). **Get
  Kiln** if you don't have it yet: the launcher opens the pack at its first start.
- the **code** and **Copy link**, to pass it on;
- **Mods and credits**: every mod with its version, author and where it comes from;
- **Versions**, with what changed in each;
- **Checks**: what Crucible's automatic checks found. Crucible checks packs automatically but does not review them;
- **Comments**.

![A pack page: cover, Open in Kiln, code, mods and credits, versions, checks](/projects/kiln/shots/crucible-web-pack.webp)

## Comments, upvotes and reports

**Sign in with Discord** at the top of the page to take part. The site keeps you signed in for 30 days, on Crucible's
pages only.

- **Comments**: write one, reply to one (one level of replies), edit or delete your own. The pack's creator is marked.
- **Upvote** (the ▲ button beside **Report**) a pack you like. Upvotes help sort the gallery.
- **Report** a pack that contains malware or harmful code, shares someone's work without credit or permission, is
  broken or offensive. Pick what is wrong, add a line if you like, and send.

![Comments on a pack page, with a reply from the pack's creator](/projects/kiln/shots/crucible-web-comments.webp)

![Report this pack: what is wrong, details, Send report](/projects/kiln/shots/crucible-web-report.webp)

Every creator has a page with their public packs: click their name on a pack.

![A creator's page with their public packs](/projects/kiln/shots/crucible-web-creator.webp)

## Rules and your data

- [Terms](/kiln/terms): share only mods you may share, credit their authors, and no malware, hidden data collection or
  ads in packs.
- [Privacy](/kiln/privacy): what Crucible keeps about you, and how to have it deleted.
- [Copyright on Crucible](/kiln/crucible/copyright): how a mod's author asks for their work to be taken down.

Make and share your own: [share your mods](/kiln/wiki/crucible-share) or [Pack Studio](/kiln/wiki/pack-studio).
