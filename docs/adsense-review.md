# AdSense follow-up

Google declined frostdev.io on October 9, 2026 for **Low value content**. Site
ownership was verified and ads.txt was Authorized. Technical verification alone
does not establish content quality or approval.

This follow-up adds five substantial guides grounded in each project's actual
documentation: Rimeward workspaces, Crosspane desk setup, Rimewire agent handoffs,
Frostsim simulations and Loothing raid preparation. Chapter navigation, animated
examples, troubleshooting and product links make the content usable and discoverable.
The hub, privacy page, robots and sitemap support the public content.

The public site now has an independent repository, static build, Website workflow
and immutable deployment. Website publication does not build or deploy Rimeward's
native app or authenticated server. No desktop tag, updater or installation is
part of this follow-up.

Before requesting another review, verify the committed site over public HTTPS,
including all guides, crawler files and privacy. Record the exact visible Google
submission result separately. A review request is not Google's approval, and
Google may still require evidence of an established audience. Do not invent
traffic or repeatedly submit an unchanged site.

Publisher metadata and ads.txt remain unchanged. No ad script or automatic ad
setting is enabled. Ad delivery and any applicable consent configuration remain
a separate setup.

References: [site readiness](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7299563),
[approval issues](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/81904), and
[privacy disclosures](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1348695).
