# Home for Thanksgiving

- **Taxonomy slug:** `stylized-concept`
- **Generation mode:** built-in `image_gen`
- **Format:** polished 16:9 concept image
- **Output:** `assets/product-marketing/glaze-looks/thanksgivinghome/concept.png`

## Final prompt

Create a polished 16:9 stylized concept illustration for a browser-extension visual theme called Home for Thanksgiving. Show a cozy modern living room just after someone has arrived home: at far left, an open front door casts a narrow wedge of warm cream light across a deep pine floor, with only a subtle suitcase or coat-edge silhouette suggesting the arrival; in the foreground, a small black cat with clear ears turns toward the doorway as the narrative reveal. In the background, a large multi-pane window shows aubergine twilight, an expressive dark tree branch, and a few copper and cranberry autumn leaves drifting outside. At right midground, a simple stone fireplace glows with restrained cream-and-copper flames and a few embers. In the foreground, include a small round side table with a steaming mug and the edge of a comfortable chair draped with a folded cranberry blanket. Build strong readable depth planes: dark foreground silhouettes, warm architectural midground, cool twilight background. Use deep pine, cranberry, warm cream, burnished copper, muted aubergine, and charcoal. Nostalgic but modern, uncluttered, cinematic editorial graphic illustration, crisp shapes with subtle painterly texture, readable at thumbnail size, no generic orange gradient. Autumn Thanksgiving atmosphere only: no Christmas decorations, no snow, no wreath, no holiday tree, no pumpkins, no turkey, no feast table. No text, logo, watermark, border, or UI.

## Production translation

The custom shader and SVG preserve the concept's three depth planes and five anchors: open door, cat, window tree, hearth, and foreground mug/blanket. The palette uses pine and aubergine as the field, with cranberry, cream, and copper reserved for story highlights. Motion is intentionally domestic and restrained: leaves drift only behind the window, flames and embers move inside the hearth, steam curls above the mug, the door spill breathes, and the cat's tail and eyes shift on long cycles.
